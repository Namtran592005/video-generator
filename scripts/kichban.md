# Kich ban TikTok 60s - "Minh thu giao ca viec lam video cho AI"

**0-5 giay**

Canh nen toi dan sang len. Mot cau chu xuat hien.

> "Neu mot ngay AI co the tu lam video thi sao?"

---

**5-15 giay**

Cac khung canh thay doi lien tuc theo loi dan: phong canh, thanh pho, anh sang, cac doan chuyen canh muot.

**Voice-over**

> "Minh khong ngoi dung tung canh. Khong cat tung doan. Khong chinh tung hieu ung."

---

**15-28 giay**

Video tiep tuc thay doi nhp nhanh hon, noi dung khop voi loi noi.

**Voice-over**

> "Minh chi mo ta y tuong. AI Agent se tu len bo cuc, tao chuyen dong va ghep moi thu thanh mot video hoan chinh."

---

**28-42 giay**

Cac canh minh hoa dung noi dung dang noi, chuyen canh muot ma.

**Voice-over**

> "Dieu thu vi khong phai la AI thay minh sang tao. Ma la no giup bien mot y tuong thanh hinh anh nhanh hon rat nhieu."

---

**42-54 giay**

Video tiep tuc trinh dien nhieu kieu canh khac nhau de chung minh kha nang thay doi theo noi dung.

**Voice-over**

> "Moi khi muon thay doi chu de, minh chi can thay doi cau mo ta. Toan bo video se duoc tao lai theo huong hoan toan moi."

---

**54-60 giay**

Man hinh dung lai o mot khung hinh dep.

**Voice-over**

> "Day moi chi la buoc khoi dau. Minh se tiep tuc thu xem AI Agent co the dua viec tao video di xa den dau."

Dong chu cuoi:

> "Ban se giao cho AI lam video ve chu de gi?"
